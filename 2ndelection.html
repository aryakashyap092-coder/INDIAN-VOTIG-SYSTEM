About The System

Our platform demonstrates how India could conduct secure online elections. With Aadhaar-based verification, AI fraud detection, and blockchain-inspired ledgers, we ensure "One Citizen, One Vote". The system is designed to be accessible to the elderly and disabled, featuring voice guidance and high-contrast modes.

From advanced device locking mechanisms that prevent multiple logins to real-time transparent result dashboards, BHARAT E-VOTE is a comprehensive solution for modern democracy. This is strictly an educational mockup.
